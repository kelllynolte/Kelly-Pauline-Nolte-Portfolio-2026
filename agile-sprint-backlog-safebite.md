The goal of this proposed sprint is to develop and verify the SafeBite dashboard component, providing users with a clear interface for viewing relevant information and navigating to available application features.

This sprint focuses on my assigned component, `Dashboard.jsx`, and the tasks needed to support its functionality.

## 4. Acceptance Criteria

The sprint work can be considered complete when:

- The dashboard meets the agreed design and functional requirements.
- Relevant information is displayed correctly when available.
- Supported navigation options work as expected.
- Applicable loading, empty and error states are handled.
- The dashboard has been tested on the supported environment.
- Outstanding issues and unfinished tasks are documented.

## 5. Dependencies and Risks

**Dependencies:**
- Access to the SafeBite codebase.
- Confirmed dashboard requirements and design.
- Availability of the required data sources and connected features.
- Access to the development and testing environment.

**Risks:**
- Required data may not be available during development.
- Other features may change while the dashboard is being developed.
- Navigation or integration issues may affect the user experience.
- Time constraints may prevent all planned tasks from being completed.

The team should review these dependencies and risks before committing to the sprint.

## 6. Sprint Review

At the end of the sprint, the team should review the completed dashboard work against the acceptance criteria. Any unfinished tasks should remain open and be considered for a future sprint.
