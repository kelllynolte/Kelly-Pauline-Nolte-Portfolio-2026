# Agile Project Charter — SafeBite

**Project Name:** SafeBite  
**Project Type:** Entrepreneurial Work-Integrated Learning (WIL) Project  
**Project Category:** HealthTech  
**Tagline:** Scan Smart. Eat Safe. Live Well.  
**Methodology:** Agile

## 1. Project Overview

SafeBite is a mobile-first HealthTech application developed using React Native. It aims to help users make informed food choices by analysing food products against their health profiles, including allergies, medical conditions, dietary preferences and medications.

The application supports barcode scanning, ingredient-label image analysis and restaurant menu scanning. Additional features include food recall alerts, family profiles, meal planning, nutrition tracking and shopkeeper inventory management.

## 2. Problem Statement

Consumers may struggle to determine whether food is suitable for their individual health needs. People with allergies, chronic conditions or medication requirements may have difficulty identifying potentially unsuitable ingredients and food–medication interactions.

Barcode scanners may also lack information about some local or unregistered products. Shopkeepers face separate challenges in monitoring stock levels and expiry dates.

SafeBite aims to address these problems by combining personalised food safety analysis with food management features in one application.

## 3. Project Vision

To develop a user-friendly mobile application that helps people make more informed food choices while supporting personalised food safety, nutrition awareness and food inventory management.

## 4. Project Objectives

- Allow users to scan barcodes and photograph ingredient labels or restaurant menus.
- Provide food safety information personalised to user health profiles.
- Identify potential allergens and food–medication interactions.
- Provide relevant food recall alerts.
- Support family profiles and personalised food recommendations.
- Offer recipe suggestions, meal planning and nutrition tracking.
- Help shopkeepers monitor inventory, stock levels and expiry dates.

## 5. Project Scope

### In Scope

- User health profiles and family profiles.
- Barcode and QR-code scanning.
- Ingredient-label and restaurant menu image analysis.
- AI-powered food safety summaries.
- Allergen matching and food–medication interaction warnings.
- Food recall monitoring and notifications.
- Recipe suggestions and meal planning.
- Nutrition tracking and food diary.
- Shopkeeper inventory and expiry management.
- Accessibility features, including read-aloud verdicts.

### Out of Scope

Features not included in the agreed project requirements, future enhancements that have not been approved, and functionality that the team has not committed to deliver within the current project scope.

## 7. Proposed Deliverables

- SafeBite mobile application.
- Food scanning and personalised safety features.
- Health profile and food management functionality.
- Shopkeeper inventory functionality.
- Project documentation.
- User stories and tasks.
- Product backlog and sprint backlog.
- Testing evidence and Agile logbook.

## 8. Agile Working Approach

The project will be organised into manageable tasks based on features and priorities. User stories will describe user needs, while the product backlog will contain planned work. The sprint backlog will identify the work selected for a particular sprint.

Progress should be recorded in the Agile logbook. The team should review completed work, identify challenges and update plans when necessary.

## 9. Risks and Considerations

- AI-generated food safety information may be inaccurate or incomplete.
- Ingredient labels or menu photographs may be unclear.
- External services may be unavailable or return incomplete data.
- Personal health information requires appropriate privacy and security controls.
- Notifications may fail or be delayed.
- Project scope may increase as new features are considered.

Potential risks should be addressed through testing, validation, clear user communication and appropriate data protection measures. SafeBite should not be presented as a replacement for professional medical advice.

## 10. Success Criteria

The project will be evaluated against the agreed requirements and the functionality implemented by the team. Success criteria include:

- Users can access the implemented food scanning features.
- Food safety results are presented clearly.
- Personalisation uses the relevant profile information where implemented.
- Inventory features support the agreed shopkeeper workflows.
- The application is tested and documented.
- Project tasks and progress are recorded accurately.
- The final deliverables meet the assessment requirements.

Developing SafeBite provides an opportunity to apply software development knowledge to a practical problem. The project highlights the importance of user-centred design, collaboration, requirements analysis, testing and responsible handling of health-related information.
