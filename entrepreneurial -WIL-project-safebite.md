## Entrepreneurial WIL Project: SafeBite

**Tagline:** Scan Smart. Eat Safe. Live Well.

### 1. Project Overview

SafeBite is a mobile-first HealthTech application developed using React Native. It helps users make more informed food choices by analysing food products against their personal health profiles, including allergies, medical conditions, dietary preferences and medications.

Users can scan product barcodes, photograph ingredients labels or scan restaurant menus to receive AI-powered food safety information. The application also includes food recall alerts, family health profiles, meal planning, nutrition tracking and inventory management for shopkeepers.

### 2. Problem Statement

Consumers can struggle to determine whether food is suitable for their individual health needs. People with allergies, chronic conditions or medication requirements may find it difficult to identify potentially unsuitable ingredients and food–medication interactions.

Traditional barcode scanners may also lack information about local or unregistered products. In addition, shopkeepers need ways to monitor stock levels and expiry dates to help reduce waste.

SafeBite aims to address these challenges by bringing personalised food safety information and food management features together in one application.

### 3. Project Objectives

- Provide several ways to analyse food, including barcode scanning, ingredient-label photographs and restaurant menu photographs.
- Personalise food safety information using a user's health profile.
- Identify potential allergen and food–medication interactions.
- Monitor food recall information and provide relevant alerts.
- Support family profiles, recipe suggestions and meal planning.
- Help users track food intake, nutrition and eating habits.
- Provide shopkeepers with inventory and expiry-management tools.

### 4. Main Features

**Food scanning and AI analysis**
- Barcode and QR-code scanning.
- Ingredient-label image analysis.
- Restaurant menu scanning with Safe, Caution and Avoid ratings.

**Personalised health and safety**
- Allergen matching and food–medication interaction warnings.
- Multiple family health profiles.
- AI health assistant called Sage, powered by Google Gemini.
- Food recall monitoring using the openFDA recall feed.

**Nutrition and meal planning**
- Recipe suggestions and healthier alternatives.
- Seven-day meal planning and shopping lists.
- Nutrition tracking, food diary and daily health score.
- Medication reminders and food-timing warnings.

**Accessibility and reporting**
- Read-aloud food safety verdicts.
- PDF health reports for sharing with healthcare professionals.
- Recently scanned products and offline scan caching.

**Shopkeeper tools**
- Product inventory management.
- Stock-level and expiry-date monitoring.
- Insights dashboard for inventory information.

### 5. Technologies and Services

Based on the project documentation:

- **React Native:** Mobile application development.
- **Google Gemini:** AI-powered conversational assistant.
- **Supabase:** Authentication, data storage and shopkeeper inventory management.
- **openFDA:** Food recall information.
- **Device notifications and local caching:** Support for alerts and offline access.
